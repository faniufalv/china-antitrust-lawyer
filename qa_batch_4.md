## Q1: [Traffic Accidents] For Expatriates: How to protect trademark rights against infringement?

**A**: Trademark infringement remedies: 1) Collect and preserve evidence including notarized purchases; 2) Send cease and desist letter; 3) File complaint with market supervision authority; 4) File lawsuit for injunction and damages; 5) Compensation based on actual losses, infringer profits, or royalty multiples.

**法律依据**: 《著作权法》

**领域**: 知识产权 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Real Estate Disputes] What legal risks should overseas Chinese be aware of when investing in China?

**A**: Overseas Chinese investing in China should be aware of: 1) Foreign Investment Negative List restrictions; 2) Foreign exchange controls requiring compliance approval for profit repatriation; 3) Tax compliance considering China tax law and bilateral tax treaties. We recommend engaging professional lawyers for comprehensive legal due diligence before investment.

**法律依据**: 《外商投资法》

**领域**: 涉外投资 | **语言**: English
